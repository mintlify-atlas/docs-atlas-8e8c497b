The dashboard is the first screen you see after logging in. It aggregates live metrics from four operational areas and surfaces the most time-sensitive information so you can act without navigating deeper into the application.

<Note>
  Sections visible on the dashboard depend on your assigned `paginasPermitidas`. Users without access to a module will not see its metric cards.
</Note>

## What the dashboard shows

The dashboard is divided into five sections: a welcome banner, and one metric group each for **Inventario**, **Ventas**, **Compras**, and **Finanzas**. Below those sits a **Acciones Rápidas** panel with direct links to the most common workflows.

### Inventario metrics

| Card | What it measures |
|------|------------------|
| **Total Productos** | Count of distinct `Producto` records across all `Tienda` locations |
| **Valor Total** | Aggregate inventory valuation in MXN |
| **Stock Bajo** | Number of products flagged as critically low |
| **En Tránsito** | Units currently in transit between locations |

Click **Ver Detalle** to navigate directly to **Inventario → Consulta de Stock**.

### Ventas metrics

| Card | What it measures |
|------|------------------|
| **Ventas Hoy** | Orders recorded today, with day-over-day comparison |
| **Ventas del Mes** | Total orders processed in the current calendar month |
| **Pendientes** | Sales awaiting inventory assignment |
| **Completadas** | Fully processed orders this month |

Click **Ver Detalle** to navigate to **Ventas → Asignación Manual**.

### Compras metrics

| Card | What it measures |
|------|------------------|
| **Órdenes Activas** | Purchase orders in `BORRADOR` or `ENVIADA` state |
| **Dañadas** | Items flagged with `cantidadDanada > 0` awaiting damage management |
| **En Revisión** | Receipts with unresolved discrepancies |
| **Valor Compras** | Total value of purchase orders this month |

Click **Ver Detalle** to navigate to **Compras → Órdenes de Compra**.

### Finanzas metrics

| Card | What it measures |
|------|------------------|
| **Ingresos del Mes** | Total revenue for the current month |
| **Egresos del Mes** | Operational costs for the current month |
| **Utilidad Neta** | Net profit (ingresos − egresos) |
| **Margen Promedio** | Blended margin percentage across all sold products |

Click **Ver Detalle** to navigate to **Finanzas → Análisis**.

## Acciones rápidas

The quick-action panel at the bottom of the dashboard provides one-click shortcuts to the most frequent workflows, rendered as icon cards:

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Consultar Stock" icon="package" href="/features/inventory">
    Jump directly to the inventory consultation page to check real-time stock levels by store location.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Asignación Manual" icon="shopping-cart" href="/features/sales">
    Open the manual inventory assignment interface to assign merchandise to pending sales.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Órdenes de Compra" icon="file-text" href="/features/purchases">
    Create or review purchase orders and check supplier delivery status.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Análisis Financiero" icon="bar-chart-3" href="/features/finances">
    Navigate to financial analysis charts including sales trends and margin breakdowns.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

<Tip>
  Each quick-action card only appears if your account has permission for the corresponding module. Contact your administrator to request access.
</Tip>

## Navigating from the dashboard

Every metric group includes a **Ver Detalle** button in the top-right corner of its section. These buttons navigate you to the relevant module page:

- **Inventario → Ver Detalle** → `/inventario/consulta`
- **Ventas → Ver Detalle** → `/ventas/asignacion-manual`
- **Compras → Ver Detalle** → `/compras/ordenes`
- **Finanzas → Ver Detalle** → `/finanzas/analisis`

You can also use the sidebar navigation on the left to go directly to any section at any time.

## Data freshness

Dashboard metrics are fetched from four API endpoints in parallel when the page loads:

- `/api/dashboard/inventario`
- `/api/dashboard/ventas`
- `/api/dashboard/compras`
- `/api/dashboard/finanzas`

The data reflects the state of the database at the moment you open or refresh the page. To see updated figures, reload the dashboard.
